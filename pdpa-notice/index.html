Personal data

PDPA Notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This notice is issued under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). It sets out the purposes for which Opty Quest collects, uses, and discloses personal data, and how individuals can reach our Data Protection Officer. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

1. Personal data collected

Opty Quest collects personal data only when someone chooses to contact us. The contact form opens an email in the visitor's own mail application; nothing is sent to our server until that email is sent. A message may include:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • the topic and content of the message.

We do not ask for NRIC or FIN numbers, Singpass details, one-time passwords, bank details, passwords, or CVs.

2. Purposes of collection, use, and disclosure

  • Reading and replying to corrections, questions, and topic suggestions.
  • Reviewing and correcting articles where a reader points out an error.
  • Keeping the website secure and running.
  • Meeting legal and regulatory requirements.

Personal data is not sold, not used for marketing without separate consent, and not used for recruitment or job placement.

3. Consent

Sending an email to Opty Quest means consenting to the collection, use, and disclosure of the personal data in it for the purposes above. Consent may be withdrawn at any time by writing to our Data Protection Officer. If consent is withdrawn, we may be unable to continue handling the enquiry.

4. Disclosure to third parties

Personal data is disclosed only to our email service provider as needed to receive and answer messages, where the law requires it, or with consent. Where that provider stores data outside Singapore, we take reasonable steps to ensure a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

5. Protection and retention

We take reasonable security arrangements to protect personal data. Messages are kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry or as required by law, and are then deleted or anonymised.

6. Access and correction

Individuals may request access to, or correction of, the personal data Opty Quest holds about them. We may need to verify identity before acting on a request and aim to respond within 30 days.

7. Data Protection Officer

  • Data Protection Officer, Opty Quest
  • support@optyquest.com
  • +65 8396 3756
  • 22 Orchard Road, #03-01, Singapore 238885

If a concern is not resolved to your satisfaction, the Personal Data Protection Commission can be reached at pdpc.gov.sg.
