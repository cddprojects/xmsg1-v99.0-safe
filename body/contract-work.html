Contract and project-based work in Singapore: reading the terms in a restructuring year

Fixed-term and project-based roles are common in technology and professional services. With reorganisation driving most retrenchments, the terms of those contracts matter more.

WNFind Aligner Editorial19 November 2026 · 2 min read

Contract work has long been a feature of Singapore's technology and professional-services sectors, where teams scale up for a system rollout, an audit cycle, or a client engagement. In 2026, it sits against a backdrop of restructuring: business reorganisation accounted for 72.1% of retrenchments in the second quarter.

A restructuring year

Professional services and information and communications both recorded resident employment gains in the second quarter, followed by subdued growth in the third. Retrenchments across the economy rose to 4,800 in the third quarter, with MOM noting they remained elevated in selected outward-oriented sectors. For firms uncertain about headcount, fixed-term and project-based arrangements are a common way to keep work moving.

Fixed-term employment and project fees

"Contract" covers two quite different arrangements. A fixed-term contract of service is employment: it ends on a set date, but Employment Act protections and CPF apply while it runs. A project fee paid under a contract for service is self-employment, with the worker responsible for their own CPF and tax position.

"Contract" can mean employment with an end date or self-employment with a fee. The difference shapes CPF, leave, and tax.

Through an agency

Many contract roles in technology and professional services are filled through staffing agencies. In that case the agency, not the client firm, is usually the employer of record — the party that pays salary and CPF. Licensed employment agencies can be checked on the Ministry of Manpower's Employment Agency Directory.

What contract terms commonly cover

  • The employer of record and the client the work is for.
  • The start and end dates, and whether the project end is also the contract end.
  • Early termination, notice, and renewal terms.
  • Leave accrual and CPF, where the role is employment.

Entry-level professional roles

Entry-level PMET vacancies stood at 31,700 in June, 45.3% of all vacancies, with professional services among the sectors represented. MOM defines the group as roles advertised with a minimum salary between $2,300 and $5,000. Some of those openings are fixed-term or project-based.
