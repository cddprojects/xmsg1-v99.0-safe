Industry note · Technology

Information and communications is still hiring, on tighter terms.

Selective, with restructuring

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

Resident employment growth in information and communications was subdued in the third quarter of 2026, alongside financial services and professional services. That is a change in tone from the second quarter, when the sector still recorded resident employment gains.

Vacancies in the sector fell from 5,300 in March 2026 to 4,400 in June. Retrenchments rose from 530 in the first quarter to 720 in the second. MOM linked higher recruitment and resignation rates in the sector with business restructuring and people moving between firms, not with a broad hiring boom.

Advertisements still mention data, cloud, cyber, and applied AI work. In a quarter shaped by restructuring, those advertisements more often describe a tool inside a real workflow than a list of product names.

How the work is usually described

Openings are fewer than a year earlier. The advertisements that remain tend to name a system, the size of the workload, and what changed. Remote days and contract terms appear in individual advertisements. They are a feature of that advertisement, and the sector as a whole still includes office-based employment.

On 16 September 2026, SWDA published a public catalogue of more than 200 AI-related courses, with a time-limited tool subscription for eligible citizens who enrol and attend. Eligibility is described on SWDA's own pages. The catalogue is a training list.

Details that often appear in advertisements

  • The product or system, and the part of the work attached to it.
  • Permanent employment, a contract, or a vendor placement, and the employer of record.
  • Office days, time-zone overlap, and on-call hours.
  • Any fee for equipment, a starter kit, or a training seat in order to be considered.

National figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026, and Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026. This note shares that public picture.
