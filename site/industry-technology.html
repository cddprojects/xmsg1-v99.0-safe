Industry guide · Technology

Information and communications is still hiring, on tighter terms.

Selective, with restructuring

Updated 19 November 2026The next official look at sectors and vacancies is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected in mid-December 2026.

What the market looks like

Resident employment growth in information and communications was slower in the third quarter of 2026, alongside financial services and professional services. That is a change in tone from the second quarter, when the sector still recorded resident employment gains.

Vacancies in the sector fell from 5,300 in March 2026 to 4,400 in June. Retrenchments rose from 530 in the first quarter to 720 in the second. MOM linked higher recruitment and resignation rates in the sector with business restructuring and people moving between firms, not with a broad hiring boom.

You will still see advertisements for data, cloud, cyber, and applied AI work. In a quarter shaped by restructuring, those advertisements tend to describe a tool used in day-to-day work, rather than a list of product names.

What the advertisements tend to say

There are fewer openings than a year earlier. The advertisements that remain tend to name a system, the size of the workload, and what changed. Some mention remote days or a contract. That is true of that advertisement. Many technology roles are still based in an office.

On 16 September 2026, SWDA published a public list of more than 200 AI-related courses, with a time-limited tool subscription for eligible citizens who enrol and attend. Who can use it is explained on SWDA's own pages. The list is courses, not jobs.

Worth looking for in an advertisement

  • The system you would work on, and which part of the work is yours.
  • Whether the role is permanent, a contract, or a placement through a vendor, and who the employer would be.
  • Office days, time-zone overlap, and on-call hours.
  • Any fee for equipment, a starter kit, or a training seat before you can be considered. Ordinary employers do not charge that.

National figures come from MOM's Labour Market Advance Release for the third quarter of 2026 and the Labour Market Report for the second quarter of 2026.

How to read the figures

  • Hours, the workplace, and the type of contract are written in each advertisement.
  • A fee just to be considered is not part of ordinary hiring.
