Industry briefing · Technology

Information and communications is still hiring, on tighter terms.

Selective, with restructuring

Updated 19 November 2026Next official sector and vacancy release: MOM Labour Market Report 3Q 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

Resident employment growth in information and communications was subdued in the third quarter of 2026, alongside financial services and professional services. That is a change in tone from the second quarter, when the sector still recorded resident employment gains.

Vacancies in the sector fell from 5,300 in March 2026 to 4,400 in June. Retrenchments rose from 530 in the first quarter to 720 in the second. MOM linked higher recruitment and resignation rates in the sector with business restructuring and people moving between firms, not with a broad hiring boom.

Advertisements still ask for data, cloud, cyber, and applied AI work. The useful distinction in an application is whether you have used a tool inside a real workflow, or whether you are listing the tool's name. Employers who are restructuring tend to ask for the first.

If you are applying

Expect fewer open roles than a year ago and more competition for the ones that remain. A short application that names systems, the size of the workload, and what changed because of your work is easier to read than a list of platforms.

Remote and contract arrangements exist in this sector. They are terms in a specific advertisement, not a feature of the industry. Ask which days are in an office, which hours overlap with the team, and whether the engagement is employment.

SWDA announced a public catalogue of more than 200 AI-related courses on 16 September 2026, with a time-limited tool subscription for eligible citizens who enrol and attend. A course from that catalogue is practice time. It is not an offer of work. Check current eligibility on SWDA's own pages.

What to check in the advertisement

  • Which product or system you worked on, and what you were accountable for.
  • Whether the role is permanent, contract, or a vendor placement, and who the employer is.
  • Office days, time zone overlap, and on-call expectations.
  • Any request to pay for equipment, a starter kit, or a training seat in order to be hired.

National figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026, and Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026. This page is general information. It is not a list of vacancies and it does not rank employers.
