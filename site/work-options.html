Work arrangements

Part-time, hybrid, and freelance work are different arrangements.

Advertisements use these words for hours, for place, or for the kind of contract. CPF, leave, and tax follow from that difference. The industry notes describe where hiring is steadier. This note describes the arrangements themselves.

ArrangementHow it is usually describedWhat usually comes with it
Full-time employeeA contract of service, commonly around a standard work week.Hours, overtime, leave, probation, and notice.
Part-time employeeUnder the Employment Act, a part-time employee is contracted to work less than 35 hours a week.The weekly hours in the contract, pay for extra hours, and pro-rated leave. The person remains an employee.
Fixed-term contractEmployment that ends on a date or when a project ends.Renewal, notice during the term, and the name of the employer.
Hybrid or work from homeAn arrangement about place. It is a schedule, and formal flexible-work requests have had a process since 1 December 2024.Which days, which hours, equipment, and whether the pattern is written into the contract.
Shift workCommon in care, transport, security, and food services.The roster, rest time, and how far ahead shifts are published.
Freelance or contract for servicePayment for a deliverable. This sits outside employment.Payment timing, ownership of the work, and insurance. IRAS and CPF Board publish how self-employed CPF and tax differ.

CPF follows employment, including part-time

A Singapore citizen or permanent resident employed under a contract of service, with total wages of more than $50 a month, is in the group whose employer pays CPF. That includes part-time, temporary, and contract employees. Where monthly wages are above $50 and not more than $500, there is no employee share, and the employer still pays the employer share. Contributions are due by the 14th of the following month, or the next working day when the 14th falls on a weekend or public holiday. Rates change, including an increase for senior employees from 1 January 2027. The current table is on the CPF Board site.

Freelance income is treated under the self-employed rules. An advertisement that uses "partner" can still be employment, or it can be a contract for service. The contract type is what the CPF position follows.

Flexible work requests

The Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests took effect on 1 December 2024. A formal request is in writing and includes the date, the type of arrangement, the reason, and the start and end where there is one. The employer has up to two months to respond. The guidelines describe that process. Agreement to the request is a separate decision. Informal arrangements can continue alongside it.

For someone still looking, the arrangement on offer is the one written in the advertisement. The formal request process applies to someone already employed.

A second stream of income

Some people add part-time or freelance work beside a main job when the hours allow it. Existing contracts sometimes include an outside-work clause, and rest-time rules still apply. Pay from a second stream depends on the hours and the work that actually comes in. These notes do not estimate earnings.

Sources: MOM Employment Act guidance on part-time employment; MOM Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests; CPF Board, saving as an employee.
