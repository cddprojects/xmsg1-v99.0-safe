Work options

Name the arrangement before you compare the pay.

Full-time, part-time, contract, hybrid, and freelance work answer different needs. The market briefings tell you where hiring is steadier. This page tells you what the arrangement itself commits you to.

Updated 19 November 2026Next official sector and vacancy release: MOM Labour Market Report 3Q 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

ArrangementWhat it usually meansWhat to confirm
Full-time employeeA contract of service, commonly around a standard work week.Hours, overtime, leave, probation, and notice.
Part-time employeeUnder the Employment Act, a part-time employee is contracted to work less than 35 hours a week.The weekly hours in the contract, how extra hours are paid, and pro-rated leave. You are still an employee.
Fixed-term contractEmployment that ends on a date or when a project ends.Whether it renews, notice during the term, and who the employer is.
Hybrid or work from homeAn arrangement about place, not a separate legal category. Formal flexible-work requests have a process from 1 December 2024.Which days, which hours, equipment, and whether the pattern is contractual or informal.
Shift workCommon in care, transport, security, and food services.The roster, rest time, and how far ahead shifts are published.
Freelance or contract for serviceYou are paid for a deliverable. This is not employment.Payment timing, who owns the work, insurance, and your own CPF and tax position. IRAS and CPF Board publish the distinctions.

CPF follows employment, including part-time

If you are a Singapore citizen or permanent resident employed under a contract of service and your total wages are more than $50 a month, your employer must pay CPF. That includes part-time, temporary, and contract employees. If you earn more than $50 and not more than $500 in a month, there is no employee share; the employer still pays the employer share. Contributions are due by the 14th of the following month, or the next working day if the 14th is a weekend or public holiday. Rates change, including an increase for senior employees from 1 January 2027. Check the current table with CPF Board.

Freelance income is treated differently. Do not let an advertisement blur the two by calling you a "partner" when the working arrangement is employment, or the reverse.

Asking for flexible work

The Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests took effect on 1 December 2024. An employee can make a formal request in writing, stating the date, the type of arrangement, the reason, and the start and end if there is one. The employer has up to two months to respond. The guidelines set out a process. They do not require the employer to agree to the request. Informal arrangements can continue without this process.

If you are still applying, ask what arrangement the advertisement is actually offering. A formal request is a step for someone already employed.

A second stream of income

Some people add part-time or freelance work beside a main job because hours allow it. Check the existing contract for outside-work clauses, and check rest-time rules, before you assume both can run together. Pay from a second stream is not guaranteed and depends on the hours you can sustain. This site does not estimate earnings.

Sources: MOM Employment Act guidance on part-time employment; MOM Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests; CPF Board, saving as an employee.
