Work types in Singapore

A six-part series on how full-time, part-time, hybrid, online, contract, and freelance work are faring in Singapore's job market at the end of 2026.

Work typeWhere it is most visibleHow it is usually structured
Full-timePublic services, care, and transport, where resident employment is growing.Contract of service around a standard week, with leave, notice, and CPF.
Part-timeCare, transport, and essential services.Fewer than 35 contracted hours a week; still employment.
HybridTechnology and financial services.A set number of office days, sometimes with fixed anchor days.
OnlineTechnology, parts of professional services, and freelance projects.Work delivered over the internet, often with overlap hours.
Contract and project-basedTechnology and professional services.Fixed-term employment, or a project fee under a contract for service.
FreelanceTechnology, finance, and professional services.Self-employment, with CPF and tax under the self-employed rules.
