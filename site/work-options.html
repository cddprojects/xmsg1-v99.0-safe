Work types · November 2026

Full-time, part-time, hybrid, online, contract, and freelance work.

Six arrangements people look for in Singapore, set against this quarter's labour figures. Full-time and part-time hiring is holding in care, transport, and public services. Hybrid, online, contract, project-based, and freelance work are more visible in technology, finance, and professional services, where vacancies are thinner.

Full-time work

Full-time work is still the largest pattern in resident employment. In the third quarter of 2026, that resident growth was supported in public administration and education, transportation and storage, and health and social services. A full-time role is a contract of service, commonly around a standard week, with hours, overtime, leave, probation, and notice written into it.

In technology and finance, vacancies were fewer by June. Financial services fell from 5,800 to 4,500, and information and communications from 5,300 to 4,400. Hiring there is selective. In care and public services it is steadier, and advertisements more often name shifts, registration, or a scheme window.

Part-time work

Part-time work, under the Employment Act, is a contract for fewer than 35 hours a week. It is still employment. Pro-rated leave and CPF for citizens and permanent residents follow from that, once monthly wages are more than $50. The current CPF table is on the CPF Board site.

Part-time hours show up most clearly in care, transport, and essential services, the same sectors supporting resident employment this quarter. Some people also keep part-time work beside a full-time role when the hours and the existing contract allow it. Pay depends on the hours that are actually rostered.

Hybrid work

Hybrid work is a schedule: some days in an office, some days elsewhere. It is common language in technology and finance advertisements. Those are also the sectors whose vacancies fell by June, and whose resident employment growth was subdued in the third quarter. A hybrid posting in a selective market still names the office days.

Formal flexible-work requests have had a written process since 1 December 2024. The employer has up to two months to respond. The guidelines describe the process. Agreement is a separate decision, and the pattern in a job advertisement is the one on offer for a new role.

Online work

Online work means the tasks are done over the internet. It appears in technology, in parts of professional services, and in freelance projects. It is a feature of those advertisements. The national figures do not publish a separate count for online jobs, and many postings that say online still include overlap hours or a few days on site.

In information and communications, vacancies were 4,400 in June, down from 5,300 in March, and retrenchments in the second quarter rose from 530 to 720. Online arrangements are still being advertised inside that tighter market.

Contract and project-based work

Contract and project-based work is employment that ends on a date, or a fee for a defined piece of work. It is common in technology and professional services. Those sectors recorded resident gains in the second quarter and subdued resident growth in the third, and business reorganisation was the main reason for retrenchments across the economy. A contract often names the employer, the end date, and whether it can end early.

Project-based professional work — a memo, a model, a drawing set, a managed account — can be employment by the firm or employment by an agency deployed to the firm. Those are different CPF positions. Entry-level PMET vacancies, 31,700 in June, included professional services among the sectors in that pool.

Freelance work

Freelance work is paid per project and sits outside employment. It shows up where firms want a defined deliverable without adding a headcount, which fits a quarter of selective hiring in technology, finance, and professional services. It also shows up beside a full-time or part-time role.

CPF and tax for freelance income follow the self-employed rules published by CPF Board and IRAS. An advertisement that says "partner" can still be employment, or it can be freelance. The contract type is what the CPF position follows. These notes do not estimate freelance earnings.

CPF follows employment, including part-time

A Singapore citizen or permanent resident employed under a contract of service, with total wages of more than $50 a month, is in the group whose employer pays CPF. That includes part-time, temporary, and contract employees. Where monthly wages are above $50 and not more than $500, there is no employee share, and the employer still pays the employer share. Contributions are due by the 14th of the following month, or the next working day when the 14th falls on a weekend or public holiday. Rates change, including an increase for senior employees from 1 January 2027. The current table is on the CPF Board site.

Sources: MOM Employment Act guidance on part-time employment; MOM Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests; CPF Board, saving as an employee; MOM labour market releases for the second and third quarters of 2026.
