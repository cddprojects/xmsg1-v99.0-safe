Industry note · Finance

Financial services is hiring selectively, not broadly.

Fewer vacancies, more restructuring

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

Financial services vacancies fell from 5,800 in March 2026 to 4,500 in June, one of the two largest sector declines in that quarter. Retrenchments in the sector rose from 560 in the first quarter to 710 in the second. Business reorganisation was the main reason for retrenchments across the economy.

In the third quarter, resident employment growth in financial services was subdued. The second quarter had still shown resident gains. Against a 2025 picture of the sector, the later figures show fewer vacancies and smaller teams in some areas, with the remaining advertisements filled more selectively.

Public descriptions of the sector still include operations, compliance, risk, finance change, and client servicing. Those are the kinds of work the sector talks about. The vacancy count says how many seats were open in June.

How the work is usually described

Advertisements that are still open often name a regulation, a product, or a process, such as reconciliations, case handling, vendor checks, or management information. Hybrid wording is common, and the number of office days is usually in the same advertisement. A role described as flexible can still require every day in the office. That schedule is part of the role.

Some client-facing and representative roles refer to a licence. The Monetary Authority of Singapore keeps public registers for those roles, and advertisements sometimes say whether a licence is required or sponsored.

Details that often appear in advertisements

  • The employer of record, including where a posting comes through an agency.
  • Any licence or certification, and which party pays for it.
  • Office days and the hours someone is expected to be reachable.
  • Pay as base salary, allowance, or commission, and what the commission depends on.

National figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026, and Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026. This note shares that public picture.
