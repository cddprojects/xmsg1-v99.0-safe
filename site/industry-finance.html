Industry guide · Finance

Financial services is hiring selectively, not broadly.

Fewer vacancies, more restructuring

Updated 19 November 2026The next official look at sectors and vacancies is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected in mid-December 2026.

What the market looks like

Financial services vacancies fell from 5,800 in March 2026 to 4,500 in June, one of the two largest sector declines in that quarter. Retrenchments in the sector rose from 560 in the first quarter to 710 in the second. Business reorganisation was the main reason for retrenchments across the economy.

In the third quarter, resident employment growth in financial services was slower. The second quarter had still shown resident gains. Against a 2025 picture of the sector, the later figures show fewer vacancies and smaller teams in some areas, with the remaining advertisements filled more selectively.

You will still see advertisements for operations, compliance, risk, finance change, and client servicing. The vacancy count is how many openings there were in June.

What the advertisements tend to say

Advertisements that are still open often name a regulation, a product, or a process, such as reconciliations, case handling, vendor checks, or management information. Hybrid wording is common, and the number of office days is usually in the same advertisement. A role described as flexible can still mean every day in the office. The advertisement is where that schedule is spelled out.

Some client-facing and representative roles refer to a licence. The Monetary Authority of Singapore keeps public registers for those roles. An advertisement sometimes says whether a licence is required, or whether the employer will sponsor it.

Worth looking for in an advertisement

  • Who the employer is, including when the advertisement comes through an agency.
  • Any licence or certification, and who pays for it.
  • How many days are in the office, and the hours you would need to be reachable.
  • Whether pay is salary, an allowance, or commission, and what the commission depends on.

National figures come from MOM's Labour Market Advance Release for the third quarter of 2026 and the Labour Market Report for the second quarter of 2026.

How to read the figures

  • They describe the country as a whole. One employer can look quite different.
  • Hours, the workplace, and the type of contract are written in each advertisement.
  • A fee just to be considered is not part of ordinary hiring.
