Industry briefing · Professional services

The second-quarter pickup did not carry straight into the third.

Cooled after earlier gains

Updated 19 November 2026Next official sector and vacancy release: MOM Labour Market Report 3Q 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

Professional services recorded resident employment gains in the second quarter of 2026, along with other outward-oriented sectors. In the third quarter, resident growth in the sector was subdued. Read both sentences together. A CV strategy built only on the earlier quarter will overstate how many seats are opening.

In June, entry-level PMET vacancies spanned sectors including professional services as well as health and social services. Those 31,700 vacancies were roles advertised with a minimum salary between $2,300 and $5,000. They are a pool for newer applicants, not a promise that every firm is hiring graduates.

The sector covers different businesses: legal, accounting, management consulting, architecture, and engineering. A slowdown in one practice does not describe the others. Look at advertisements in the specific practice, not at the sector label alone.

If you are applying

Professional-services applications are often won or lost on evidence of client work: a memo, a model, a drawing set, a managed account. Describe the deliverable and your part in it. Titles such as "associate" mean different things in different firms.

Project-based contracts are common. Ask the length, whether the contract can end early, and whether you are an employee of the firm or of a staffing company. Those are different pay and CPF situations.

If you are changing career into this sector, the skills page covers how to compare a SkillsFuture course with the tasks in current ads before you enrol.

What to check in the advertisement

  • Employee of the firm, or employed by an agency and deployed to the firm.
  • Utilisation or billing expectations, if the advertisement mentions them.
  • Qualification route for regulated work, such as accounting or engineering, confirmed with the professional body.
  • Whether the project end date is the employment end date.
