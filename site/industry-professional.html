Professional services jobs in Singapore: a cooler quarter after earlier gains

Legal, accounting, consulting, and engineering services added resident jobs in the second quarter, then slowed in the third. Project-based contracts are a large part of the picture.

By WorkNest SG Editorial19 November 2026 · 2 min readOutlook: Mixed

Work types most often seen: project-based contracts, hybrid, and some freelance

Where the sector stands

Professional services recorded resident employment gains in the second quarter of 2026, along with other outward-oriented sectors. In the third quarter, resident growth in the sector was subdued, leaving a cooler picture than the second quarter alone suggested.

In June, entry-level PMET vacancies included professional services alongside health and social services. Those 31,700 vacancies covered roles advertised with a minimum salary between $2,300 and $5,000, a statistical grouping that spans many kinds of firms.

The sector covers legal, accounting, management consulting, architecture, and engineering. Conditions can differ sharply between practices, and advertisements in a specific field carry more information than the sector label.

How roles are usually advertised

Advertisements often refer to a specific piece of client work: an audit, a model, a set of drawings, a managed account. Titles such as "associate" vary in meaning from firm to firm. Project-based contracts are common, and the contract length, early-termination terms, and employer — the firm itself or a staffing company — shape the pay and CPF position.

Details commonly found in postings

  • Whether the employer is the firm itself or an agency placing staff at the firm.
  • Utilisation or billing targets, where mentioned.
  • The qualification route for regulated work, as set by the professional body.
  • Whether the end of a project also ends the employment.

Sources

  • Ministry of Manpower, Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026 (30 October 2026)
  • Ministry of Manpower, Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026 (21 September 2026)
