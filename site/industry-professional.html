Industry note · Professional services

The second-quarter pickup did not carry straight into the third.

Cooled after earlier gains

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

Professional services recorded resident employment gains in the second quarter of 2026, along with other outward-oriented sectors. In the third quarter, resident growth in the sector was subdued. The two quarters together show a cooler market than the second quarter on its own.

In June, entry-level PMET vacancies spanned sectors including professional services and health and social services. Those 31,700 vacancies were roles advertised with a minimum salary between $2,300 and $5,000. The pool is a statistical group. It describes advertised minimums, and firms differ inside it.

The sector covers legal, accounting, management consulting, architecture, and engineering. A slow quarter in one practice can sit next to a steadier one. Advertisements in the specific practice carry more detail than the sector label.

How the work is usually described

Advertisements in this sector often refer to a piece of client work: a memo, a model, a drawing set, a managed account. Titles such as "associate" mean different things from one firm to another. Project-based contracts are common, and the length, early-end terms, and the employer — the firm itself, or a staffing company — change the pay and CPF position.

The skills note sets out SkillsFuture Credit and the public course list that sit beside these advertisements.

Details that often appear in advertisements

  • Employment by the firm, or employment by an agency with deployment to the firm.
  • Utilisation or billing expectations, where the advertisement mentions them.
  • The qualification route for regulated work such as accounting or engineering, as published by the professional body.
  • Whether a project end date is also the employment end date.

National figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026, and Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026. This note shares that public picture.
