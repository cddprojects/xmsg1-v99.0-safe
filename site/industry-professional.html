Industry guide · Professional services

The second-quarter pickup did not carry straight into the third.

Cooled after earlier gains

Updated 19 November 2026The next official look at sectors and vacancies is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected in mid-December 2026.

What the market looks like

Professional services recorded resident employment gains in the second quarter of 2026, along with other outward-oriented sectors. In the third quarter, resident growth in the sector was slower. The two quarters together show a cooler market than the second quarter on its own.

In June, entry-level PMET vacancies included professional services and health and social services. Those 31,700 vacancies were roles advertised with a minimum salary between $2,300 and $5,000. That groups many kinds of roles by the salary floor in the advertisement. Individual firms still differ.

The sector covers legal, accounting, management consulting, architecture, and engineering. A slow quarter in one practice can sit next to a steadier one. An advertisement in the practice you are looking at will tell you more than the sector label.

What the advertisements tend to say

Advertisements here often refer to a piece of client work: a memo, a model, a drawing set, a managed account. Titles such as "associate" mean different things from one firm to another. Project-based contracts are common. The length, whether it can end early, and who employs you — the firm itself, or a staffing company — change the pay and the CPF position.

The skills and training guide explains SkillsFuture Credit and the public course list that sit beside these advertisements.

Worth looking for in an advertisement

  • Whether you would be employed by the firm, or by an agency placed at the firm.
  • Any utilisation or billing target, if the advertisement mentions one.
  • The qualification path for regulated work such as accounting or engineering, as the professional body describes it.
  • Whether the project end date is also the end of the employment.

National figures come from MOM's Labour Market Advance Release for the third quarter of 2026 and the Labour Market Report for the second quarter of 2026.

How to read the figures

  • They describe the country as a whole. One employer can look quite different.
  • Hours, the workplace, and the type of contract are written in each advertisement.
  • A fee just to be considered is not part of ordinary hiring.
