Hybrid work in Singapore: office-day arrangements in a selective market

Hybrid has become standard language in technology and finance advertising. In 2026, that language sits in a market with fewer openings.

WNWorkNest SG Editorial19 November 2026 · 2 min read

Few phrases appear more often in Singapore's technology and financial-services job advertisements than "hybrid working". The arrangement has outlasted the pandemic years and settled into a familiar pattern. What has changed in 2026 is the market around it.

The market around hybrid roles

The sectors where hybrid arrangements are most common are also the ones where hiring has tightened. Financial services vacancies fell from 5,800 in March to 4,500 in June, and information and communications from 5,300 to 4,400. Resident employment growth in both sectors, along with professional services, was subdued in the third quarter, and retrenchments rose in the second.

Hybrid roles are still being advertised. In a selective market, though, they tend to be more precise about expectations.

What hybrid usually means in practice

Most hybrid roles specify a number of office days a week, sometimes with fixed "anchor" days when a whole team is expected in. Others set core hours during which staff need to be reachable. The word "flexible" in an advertisement can mean hybrid days, but it can also refer to staggered hours or compressed weeks, so the details in the posting are the meaningful part.

In a tighter market, hybrid advertisements are becoming more precise about office days, not less.

The formal request framework

Since 1 December 2024, the Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests have set out how employees can make a formal request. The request is made in writing and covers the arrangement sought, the reason, and the intended start and end dates. Employers are expected to consider requests properly and respond within two months, and they may decline on reasonable business grounds.

The framework applies to people already in a job. For a new role, the arrangement on offer is the one described in the advertisement and the contract.

What hybrid advertisements commonly state

  • The number of office days, and whether any are fixed.
  • Core hours or time-zone overlap for regional teams.
  • Equipment provided for work away from the office.
  • Whether the arrangement is part of the contract or a team practice.
