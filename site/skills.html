SkillsFuture Credit at the end of 2026: amounts, the 1 December change, and the AI course list

Credit amounts are unchanged, but the mid-career course list expands from 1 December, and SWDA has published a public catalogue of AI-related courses.

WNWorkNest SG Editorial19 November 2026 · 2 min read

With hiring more selective in several sectors, advertisements increasingly name a specific skill, system, or registration. Singapore's public training credits are designed to sit alongside that kind of market, and two changes in the second half of 2026 are worth noting.

The credit amounts

Singapore citizens aged 25 and above receive an opening SkillsFuture Credit of $500, which can be used on courses listed on the MySkillsFuture portal and does not expire. Citizens aged 40 and above also receive a mid-career credit of $4,000 for selected courses, which likewise does not expire.

What changes on 1 December 2026

From 1 December 2026, new course runs of full Workforce Skills Qualifications and selected courses from sector agencies join the list of courses eligible for the mid-career credit. Balances and eligible courses are shown on MySkillsFuture after a Singpass login.

A new agency and a new course list

The Skills and Workforce Development Agency, formed from SkillsFuture Singapore and Workforce Singapore, began operating on 1 July 2026. On 16 September 2026 it published a public catalogue of more than 200 AI-related courses, with a time-limited tool subscription for eligible citizens who enrol and attend. The catalogue is a training list; completing a course and receiving a job offer are separate matters.

SkillsFuture Career Transition Programmes take a train-and-place approach for sectors with hiring demand. Places, sectors, and outcomes vary between runs, and each programme page explains what placement means for that intake.

Credits are claimed through the official portal. No legitimate service asks for a Singpass login or a fee to unlock them.

Where the official information is published

  • MySkillsFuture for courses, credit balances, and subsidies.
  • Skills and Workforce Development Agency for programmes and announcements.
  • Connect by SWDA at the Lifelong Learning Institute, a public centre for career and skills advice.
  • MyCareersFuture, the government career portal.

Sources

  • MySkillsFuture, SkillsFuture Credit
