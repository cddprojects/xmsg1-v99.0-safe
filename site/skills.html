Skills and training

Train for a gap you can see in current ads.

Start from the industry briefing and two or three advertisements. A course is worth considering when the same missing skill shows up more than once. A course is not a job offer.

Updated 19 November 2026Next official sector and vacancy release: MOM Labour Market Report 3Q 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

Use the market as the filter

In a cautious sector such as technology or finance, employers are hiring fewer people and asking for evidence of work already done. A new certificate helps most when the advertisements ask for that skill and your examples are thin. In care and essential services, the filter is often a registration, a language, or a shift pattern, not another general digital badge.

SkillsFuture Credit

Singapore citizens aged 25 and above have an opening SkillsFuture Credit of $500. It can be used on courses listed on the MySkillsFuture portal and does not expire. Citizens aged 40 and above also have a mid-career credit of $4,000 for selected courses, which does not expire. From 1 December 2026, new course runs of full Workforce Skills Qualifications and selected sector-agency courses join the mid-career list. Balances and course eligibility are on MySkillsFuture after a Singpass login. Check them there before you pay a provider who says they can "claim it for you".

Where to look first

  • MySkillsFuture for courses, credit, and subsidies.
  • Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA), the statutory board formed from SkillsFuture Singapore and Workforce Singapore, in operation from 1 July 2026.
  • Connect by SWDA at the Lifelong Learning Institute, for in-person career and skills advice.
  • MyCareersFuture, the government career portal, which also flags some roles tied to public career schemes.

SkillsFuture Career Transition Programmes are train-and-place programmes aimed at sectors with hiring opportunities. Places, sectors, and outcomes change. Completing a programme is not the same as receiving an offer. Read the current programme page, including what "place" means and what happens if a match is not made.

Before you pay anyone

  • Is the same course, or a close one, listed on MySkillsFuture?
  • What can you do at the end that an advertisement in your sector currently asks for?
  • Who awards the certificate, and is a regulator involved?
  • Are you being asked for Singpass, a one-time password, or an upfront "registration fee" to unlock a government credit? Stop if you are. Credits are claimed through the official portal.

Credit amounts and the 1 December 2026 mid-career course change are from SkillsFuture Credit guidance on MySkillsFuture. Confirm your own balance. This page is not training advice for your circumstances.
