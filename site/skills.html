Skills and training

Courses and credits you can look at while you search.

In a quarter when some sectors are hiring more carefully, advertisements tend to name a specific skill, a registration, or a shift pattern. Public credits and course lists show what is available around those skills.

Updated 19 November 2026The next official look at sectors and vacancies is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected in mid-December 2026.

What employers are mentioning

In technology and finance, where vacancies fell and resident growth was slower, advertisements more often describe work you have already done on a named system or process. In care and essential services, they more often mention a registration, a language, or a shift pattern.

SkillsFuture Credit

Singapore citizens aged 25 and above have an opening SkillsFuture Credit of $500. You can use it on courses listed on MySkillsFuture, and it does not expire. Citizens aged 40 and above also have a mid-career credit of $4,000 for selected courses, which does not expire. From 1 December 2026, new runs of full Workforce Skills Qualifications and selected sector-agency courses join that mid-career list.

Your balance and which courses you can use it on are on MySkillsFuture, after a Singpass login. If someone offers to claim the credit for you and asks for your Singpass, a one-time password, or a fee up front, that is not how the official page works. You claim the credit yourself on the portal.

Where the public lists are

  • MySkillsFuture for courses, credit, and subsidies.
  • Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA), the statutory board formed from SkillsFuture Singapore and Workforce Singapore, in operation from 1 July 2026.
  • Connect by SWDA at the Lifelong Learning Institute, a public centre where you can talk about careers and skills.
  • MyCareersFuture, the government career portal, which also marks some roles tied to public career schemes.

SkillsFuture Career Transition Programmes are train-and-place programmes aimed at sectors that are hiring. Places, sectors, and results change from one run to the next. The programme page explains what "place" means that time, including what happens if a match is not made. Finishing a programme and receiving an offer from an employer are two different things.

What a course page usually tells you

  • Whether the same course, or a close one, is listed on MySkillsFuture.
  • The skill the course covers, and whether current advertisements in that sector mention it.
  • Who awards the certificate, and whether a regulator is involved.
  • Any request for Singpass, a one-time password, or a fee in order to unlock a government credit. The official route does not work that way.

The credit amounts and the 1 December 2026 mid-career course change come from SkillsFuture Credit guidance on MySkillsFuture. Your own balance is on that portal.
