Skills and training

Training sits beside the market, in public catalogues.

In a quarter when some sectors are hiring more carefully, advertisements tend to name a specific skill, a registration, or a shift pattern. Public credits and course lists describe what is available around those skills.

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

What the market is asking for

In technology and finance, where vacancies fell and resident growth was subdued, advertisements more often describe work already done on a named system or process. In care and essential services, advertisements more often mention a registration, a language, or a shift pattern.

SkillsFuture Credit

Singapore citizens aged 25 and above have an opening SkillsFuture Credit of $500. It can be used on courses listed on the MySkillsFuture portal, and it does not expire. Citizens aged 40 and above also have a mid-career credit of $4,000 for selected courses, which does not expire. From 1 December 2026, new course runs of full Workforce Skills Qualifications and selected sector-agency courses join the mid-career list.

Balances and course eligibility appear on MySkillsFuture with a Singpass login. A message that offers to "claim the credit" in exchange for a Singpass login, a one-time password, or an upfront fee sits outside that official page. Claims go through the portal itself.

Public places the lists live

  • MySkillsFuture for courses, credit, and subsidies.
  • Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA), the statutory board formed from SkillsFuture Singapore and Workforce Singapore, in operation from 1 July 2026.
  • Connect by SWDA at the Lifelong Learning Institute, a public centre for career and skills conversations.
  • MyCareersFuture, the government career portal, which also marks some roles tied to public career schemes.

SkillsFuture Career Transition Programmes are train-and-place programmes aimed at sectors with hiring opportunities. Places, sectors, and outcomes change. The current programme page describes what "place" means in that run, including the position when a match is not made. Completion of a programme and an employer's offer are separate events.

What usually sits on a course page

  • Whether the same course, or a close one, is listed on MySkillsFuture.
  • The skill the course covers, and whether current advertisements in that sector mention it.
  • The body that awards the certificate, and whether a regulator is involved.
  • Any request for Singpass, a one-time password, or a fee in order to unlock a government credit.

Credit amounts and the 1 December 2026 mid-career course change come from SkillsFuture Credit guidance on MySkillsFuture. A personal balance is on that portal.
