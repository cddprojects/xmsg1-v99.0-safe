Industry note · Public and essential services

This is where much of the resident employment growth sat in the third quarter.

Supporting resident employment

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

MOM's third-quarter advance release said resident employment growth continued to be supported mainly by essential and public services, naming public administration and education, transportation and storage, and health and social services. This note covers the public-administration and education part. Care and transport have their own pages because the day-to-day work is different.

Hiring here often follows budget cycles, schemes, and fixed windows, rather than a rolling private-sector advertisement. A quiet week on a job portal can simply be the gap between windows.

Second-quarter retrenchment increases were concentrated elsewhere. The published figures for public administration and education were quiet on that measure. A single agency can still differ from the sector total.

How the work is usually described

Scheme details usually live on the agency's own site or on Careers@Gov, as well as on any repost. Citizenship conditions, medical or security checks, and closing dates are commonly part of those pages.

Education roles can involve a qualification, a registration, or both. Early-childhood and school roles are different schemes. Eligibility pages on the relevant agency describe what a certificate is for. Mid-career schemes sometimes recognise occupational experience and sometimes use a trainee pay point. The advertisement is where that distinction is written.

Details that often appear in advertisements

  • The closing date, and whether late applications are accepted.
  • Citizenship or residency conditions.
  • Registration, medical, or security clearance, where the scheme includes them.
  • The pay point for mid-career entrants, where the scheme publishes one.

National figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026, and Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026. This note shares that public picture.
