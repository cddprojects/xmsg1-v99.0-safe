Public and essential services jobs in Singapore: where resident growth is concentrated

Public administration and education led resident employment growth in the third quarter. Hiring here tends to follow schemes and recruitment windows.

WNWorkNest SG Editorial19 November 2026 · 2 min readOutlook: Steady

Work types most often seen: full-time schemes, with some part-time

Where the sector stands

The Ministry of Manpower's third-quarter advance release said resident employment growth continued to be supported mainly by essential and public services, naming public administration and education, transportation and storage, and health and social services. This report covers public administration and education; care and transport are covered separately.

Hiring in this sector often follows budget cycles, schemes, and fixed application windows rather than rolling advertisements. A quiet period on a job portal can simply reflect the gap between windows.

The second-quarter rise in retrenchments was concentrated in other sectors, and public administration and education were not among them.

How roles are usually advertised

Scheme details are typically published on the agency's own website or on Careers@Gov. Citizenship requirements, medical or security checks, and closing dates are commonly part of those listings.

Education roles may require a qualification, a registration, or both, and early-childhood and school roles fall under different schemes. Mid-career schemes sometimes recognise prior work experience and sometimes use a trainee pay point, which the advertisement specifies.

Details commonly found in postings

  • The closing date and whether late applications are considered.
  • Citizenship or residency requirements.
  • Registration, medical, or security clearance requirements.
  • The pay point for mid-career entrants, where published.

Sources

  • Ministry of Manpower, Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026 (30 October 2026)
  • Ministry of Manpower, Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026 (21 September 2026)
