Freelance work in Singapore: project work, CPF, and tax outside employment

Freelancing fits a market in which firms want defined pieces of work without adding headcount. It also comes with its own rules on CPF, tax, and contracts.

WNWorkNest SG Editorial19 November 2026 · 2 min read

Freelance work in Singapore spans design, writing, software, consulting, tutoring, and a wide range of professional services. It is paid per project or per engagement, and it sits outside employment. In a year of selective hiring, it is one of the ways firms continue to get work done without adding permanent headcount.

Freelancing in a selective market

Technology, financial services, and professional services all saw subdued resident employment growth in the third quarter of 2026, after fewer vacancies and higher retrenchments earlier in the year. Those are also the sectors that commission much of Singapore's project-based freelance work. MOM's September poll showed hiring expectations improving but firms remaining selective.

Self-employed status

A freelancer works under a contract for service rather than a contract of service. Employment Act protections do not apply in the same way, and CPF and income tax follow the self-employed rules published by CPF Board and IRAS. Self-employed persons with annual net trade income above $6,000 make their own MediSave contributions, calculated after IRAS assesses their income.

Delivery and ride-hail workers on digital platforms are covered by a separate framework. The Platform Workers Act, in force since 1 January 2025, provides for CPF contributions and work injury compensation for that group.

A job title that says "partner" or "associate" can be employment or freelance. The contract, not the title, decides.

Freelancing alongside a main job

Some freelancers take on projects alongside a full-time or part-time role. Whether that is possible depends on the main contract, including any outside-work or conflict-of-interest clauses. Income from freelance work varies with the projects that come in, and this blog does not estimate earnings.

What freelance agreements commonly cover

The Tripartite Standard on Contracting with Self-Employed Persons sets out practices such as written agreements and timely payment. Agreements that follow it usually include:

  • The scope of work and deliverables.
  • The fee, payment schedule, and invoicing terms.
  • Ownership of the work produced.
  • What happens if the project is cancelled or changed.
