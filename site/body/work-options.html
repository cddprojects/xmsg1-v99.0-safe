Work types in Singapore

A six-part series on how full-time, part-time, hybrid, online, contract, and freelance work are faring in Singapore's job market at the end of 2026.

Work typeWhere it is most visibleHow it is usually structured
Full-timePublic services, care, and transport, where resident employment is growing.Contract of service around a standard week, with leave, notice, and CPF.
Part-timeCare, transport, and essential services.Fewer than 35 contracted hours a week; still employment.
HybridTechnology and financial services.A set number of office days, sometimes with fixed anchor days.
OnlineTechnology, parts of professional services, and freelance projects.Work delivered over the internet, often with overlap hours.
Contract and project-basedTechnology and professional services.Fixed-term employment, or a project fee under a contract for service.

Part 1 of 6

Full-time work in Singapore: where hiring is holding at the end of 2026

Full-time employment remains the backbone of resident jobs. This year, which sectors are doing the hiring matters as much as how much hiring there is.

Where it is most visible
Public services, care, and transport, where resident employment is still growing.
How it is usually set up
A contract of service around a standard week, with leave, notice, and CPF for citizens and permanent residents.

Part 2 of 6

Part-time work in Singapore: hours, CPF, and the sectors still hiring

Part-time work has a clear legal definition in Singapore, and in 2026 it is concentrated in the same sectors that are supporting resident employment.

Where it is most visible
Care, transport and storage, and essential services.
How it is usually set up
Fewer than 35 contracted hours a week. Still employment, with pro-rated leave and CPF once wages pass $50 a month.
Read the part-time article
Under 35Contracted hours a week that define a part-time employeeSource: Ministry of Manpower and CPF Board

Part 3 of 6

Hybrid work in Singapore: office-day arrangements in a selective market

Hybrid has become standard language in technology and finance advertising. In 2026, that language sits in a market with fewer openings.

Where it is most visible
Technology and financial services.
How it is usually set up
A set number of office days, sometimes with fixed anchor days. Formal requests follow the Tripartite Guidelines in force since 1 December 2024.
Read the hybrid article
4,500Financial services vacancies in June 2026, down from 5,800Source: Ministry of Manpower and CPF Board

Part 4 of 6

Online work in Singapore: how remote roles are being advertised in 2026

There is no official count of online jobs in Singapore. The closest guide is the sector data — and the warnings about online job scams.

Where it is most visible
Technology, parts of professional services, and freelance projects.
How it is usually set up
Work delivered over the internet, often with overlap hours or occasional office days.

Part 5 of 6

Contract and project-based work in Singapore: reading the terms in a restructuring year

Fixed-term and project-based roles are common in technology and professional services. With reorganisation driving most retrenchments, the terms of those contracts matter more.

Where it is most visible
Technology and professional services.
How it is usually set up
Fixed-term employment with an end date, or a project fee under a contract for service.

72.1%Share of second-quarter retrenchments due to reorganisationSource: Ministry of Manpower and CPF Board

Part 6 of 6

Freelance work in Singapore: project work, CPF, and tax outside employment

Freelancing fits a market in which firms want defined pieces of work without adding headcount. It also comes with its own rules on CPF, tax, and contracts.

Where it is most visible
Technology, finance, and professional services.
How it is usually set up
Self-employment, with MediSave and income tax handled under the self-employed rules.
