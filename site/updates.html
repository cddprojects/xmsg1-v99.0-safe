Update log

What we revised, and why.

Industry pages change when an official labour release changes the picture. Each page keeps the date of the release it follows.

  • 19 November 2026

    First set of market and industry guides. National employment, unemployment, retrenchment, and hiring intentions follow MOM's Labour Market Advance Release for the third quarter of 2026, released on 30 October 2026. Vacancy counts, the entry-level PMET definition, second-quarter retrenchment by sector, and the six-month re-entry rate follow the Labour Market Report for the second quarter of 2026, released on 21 September 2026.

    SkillsFuture Credit amounts, the 1 December 2026 mid-career course change, SWDA's start on 1 July 2026, and the Tripartite flexible-work guidelines come from those agencies' public pages. They are rechecked when those pages change.

    The Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026 is the next release MOM has pointed to, in mid-December 2026. It is expected to refresh sector splits, vacancies, turnover, and re-entry. Vacancy figures on this site remain labelled as June 2026 until then.
