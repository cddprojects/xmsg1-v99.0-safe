Update log

What we revised, and why.

Industry pages move when an official labour release changes the picture. Older pages stay dated so you can see what they were based on.

  • 19 November 2026

    Published the career assist path and the first set of industry briefings. National employment, unemployment, retrenchment, and hiring intentions follow MOM's Labour Market Advance Release for the third quarter of 2026, released on 30 October 2026. Vacancy counts, the entry-level PMET definition, second-quarter retrenchment by sector, and the six-month re-entry rate follow the Labour Market Report for the second quarter of 2026, released on 21 September 2026.

    SkillsFuture Credit amounts, the 1 December 2026 mid-career course change, SWDA's start on 1 July 2026, and the Tripartite flexible-work guidelines are taken from those agencies' public pages and will be rechecked when those pages change.

    Waiting on the Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, which MOM said would be released in mid-December 2026. That report is expected to refresh sector splits, vacancies, turnover, and re-entry. Vacancy figures on this site stay labelled as June 2026 until then.
