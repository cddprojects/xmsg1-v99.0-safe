Revisions and the releases behind them

Articles are revised when an official release changes the picture. Each entry records what changed and why.

  • 19 November 2026

    Launch of the blog, including the quarterly market review, the six-part work types series, six industry reports, and two practical guides.

    National employment, unemployment, retrenchment, and hiring-intention figures follow MOM's Labour Market Advance Release for the third quarter of 2026 (30 October 2026). Vacancy counts, the entry-level PMET definition, second-quarter retrenchment by sector, and re-entry rates follow the Labour Market Report for the second quarter of 2026 (21 September 2026).

    SkillsFuture Credit amounts, the 1 December 2026 mid-career course change, SWDA's commencement on 1 July 2026, and the Tripartite Guidelines on flexible work requests are drawn from those agencies' public pages.

    Next scheduled review: MOM's Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected in mid-December 2026.
