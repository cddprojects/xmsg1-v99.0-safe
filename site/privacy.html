Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

WorkNest SG ("we", "us") handles personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This policy explains what happens when you use this website.

1. Who we are

WorkNest SG is an independent publication about Singapore's job market. It is not an employment agency and does not collect CVs for placement.

2. Personal data we collect

The contact form does not send data to our server. It opens an email in your own mail application. A message may include your name, email address, and the content you choose to write. We do not ask for NRIC numbers, Singpass details, one-time passwords, bank details, or passwords.

3. Technical data

A web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, to keep the site secure and running. This site does not set advertising cookies and does not load third-party fonts or trackers.

4. How we use personal data

  • To read and reply to corrections, questions, and suggestions.
  • To keep the website secure.
  • To meet legal requirements.

We do not sell personal data and do not add anyone to a mailing list without separate consent.

5. Disclosure

We disclose personal data to an email service provider only as needed to receive and answer messages, where the law requires it, or with your consent.

6. Transfers

If a provider stores email outside Singapore, we take reasonable steps to ensure the data receives protection comparable to the PDPA.

7. Protection and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal data we hold. Email is not perfectly secure. We keep messages only as long as needed to handle the enquiry or as required by law, then delete or anonymise them.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, or correction of, personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent, by contacting our Data Protection Officer. We may need to verify your identity. If consent is withdrawn, we may be unable to continue handling the enquiry.

9. Other websites

Links to MOM, SWDA, CPF Board, MyCareersFuture, and other sites lead away from WorkNest SG. Their privacy practices are their own.

10. Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

The current version of this policy is the one on this page, with the date shown above.
