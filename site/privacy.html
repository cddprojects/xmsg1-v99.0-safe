Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

WorkNest SG ("we", "us") handles personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This policy explains what happens when you use this website.

1. Who we are

WorkNest SG is an independent information website. It publishes a job-market guide and industry guides. It is not an employment agency and it does not collect CVs for placement.

2. Personal data we collect

The contact form does not send data to our server. It opens an email in your own mail application. A message can include your name, your email address, and what you wanted to tell us. We do not ask for NRIC numbers, Singpass details, one-time passwords, bank details, or passwords.

3. Technical data

A web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, to keep the site secure and running. This site does not set advertising cookies and does not load third-party fonts or trackers.

4. How we use personal data

  • To read and reply to corrections, questions, and topic suggestions.
  • To keep the website secure.
  • To meet a legal requirement.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for a mailing list unless you have agreed to that separately.

5. Disclosure

We disclose personal data to a service provider who hosts email only as needed to receive and answer messages, where the law requires it, or with your consent.

6. Transfers

If a provider stores mail outside Singapore, we take reasonable steps to see that the data has protection comparable to the PDPA.

7. Protection and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data we hold. Email is not perfectly secure. We keep messages only as long as needed to handle the enquiry or as the law requires, then delete or anonymise them.

8. Your rights

You may ask for access to personal data we hold about you, ask for a correction, or withdraw consent. Email the Data Protection Officer. We may need to verify your identity. If you withdraw consent, we may not be able to continue the enquiry.

9. Other websites

Links to MOM, SWDA, CPF Board, MyCareersFuture, and others leave this site. Their privacy practices are their own.

10. Children

This site is a general audience site and is not directed at children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal data and we will delete it.

11. Changes

The current policy is this page, with the date above.
