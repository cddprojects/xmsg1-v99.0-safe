Applying

Send a specific application through a channel you can check.

WorkNest SG does not receive CVs and does not forward them. You apply to the employer, or through a public portal such as MyCareersFuture.

Updated 19 November 2026Next official sector and vacancy release: MOM Labour Market Report 3Q 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

Before you write

  1. Read the industry briefing so the application matches the current situation. A technology application written as if hiring were wide open will sound out of date.
  2. Pick two advertisements you would actually accept, including the hours and the workplace.
  3. List three tasks you have done that match duties in those advertisements. Use the tasks, not only the old job title.

What to put in

Keep it to a page unless the employer asks for more. Open with the role you are applying for. Then give the three tasks, each with a scale: how often, for whom, and what changed. Close with the arrangement you can work: days, hours, and start date. Leave out a photograph unless the employer asks for one. Leave out NRIC, passport, bank details, and date of birth.

Where to send it

Prefer the employer's own careers page. MyCareersFuture, run by SWDA with GovTech, is free to browse without Singpass. Applying there requires Singpass and is for citizens and permanent residents. A repost on another board can be useful as a pointer. It is not, by itself, confirmation that the role exists. If you cannot find the role on the employer's site or on MyCareersFuture, ask the employer through a published company address before you invest more time.

Read the advertisement for these

  • Legal name of the employer, not only a brand or a room in a messaging app.
  • Employment or freelance, hours, workplace, and pay basis: salary, hourly, or per project.
  • Closing date, if any, and the documents they actually ask for.
  • Any cost to you. A genuine employer does not charge you to apply, to interview, or to "release" an offer.

Stop, and check, if you are asked for

  • Payment, a starter kit you must buy, or a cheque you must bank and partly return.
  • Singpass login, a one-time password, or your NRIC photo at the first conversation.
  • Bank details before a written offer from an employer you have verified.
  • An interview that happens only inside a personal messaging app, with no company email or published phone number.

The ScamShield helpline is 1799. If you have lost money, report it to the Police. ACRA's business-profile search is one way to see whether a company name exists. A real registration does not make an offer genuine, and a missing registration is a reason to pause.

After you send it

Keep a simple log: employer, role, date, arrangement offered, and where you found it. Most applications do not produce an interview. That is ordinary, especially in sectors where MOM has described hiring as selective. Change the application when the advertisements are asking for something you have not shown. Do not pay a third party who promises interviews.

Nothing on this page is legal advice. Employment Act, CPF, and flexible-work details should be confirmed with MOM, CPF Board, or TAFEP when a real offer is in front of you.
