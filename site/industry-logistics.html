Industry note · Logistics and industry

Transport supported resident jobs. Manufacturing's growth was mostly non-resident.

Split between resident transport jobs and industrial hiring

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

Transportation and storage was one of the sectors supporting resident employment growth in the third quarter of 2026, together with public administration, education, and health and social services. That includes land transport, warehousing, and parts of the supply chain, not every job with "logistics" in the title.

Manufacturing is a different picture. Non-resident employment drove most of the economy's employment increase in the third quarter, mainly in construction and manufacturing. In the second quarter, manufacturing retrenchments rose from 670 to 870. Construction's employment growth is largely work-permit hiring. A large total-employment number there is not a count of resident vacancies.

June vacancy figures for these sectors are due for a refresh in the mid-December report. Until then, the employment and retrenchment split above is the later evidence.

How the work is usually described

Advertisements in transport and warehousing often turn on licence class, shift, and site. In manufacturing, production-operator roles and technician or engineer roles are different advertisements. Retrenchment has been higher here than in care or public services, and contract length is often part of the same posting.

Safety orientation and site access sit inside the job. A request for payment for a "site pass" before a named employer has confirmed the role in writing is outside that ordinary pattern. The employer's own channel is where the role, if it is real, also appears.

Details that often appear in advertisements

  • The licence or certificate named in the advertisement.
  • Shift hours, overtime practice, and the work site.
  • Employment, daily-rated work, or a freelance arrangement.
  • Which party issues any pass or permit, and whether a fee is attached.

National figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026, and Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026. This note shares that public picture.
