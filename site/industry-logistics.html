Logistics, transport, and manufacturing jobs in Singapore: a split picture

Transport and storage supported resident employment growth. Manufacturing's growth was mostly non-resident, alongside higher retrenchments.

By WorkNest SG Editorial19 November 2026 · 2 min readOutlook: Mixed

Work types most often seen: full-time and part-time site and shift work

Where the sector stands

Transportation and storage was one of the sectors supporting resident employment growth in the third quarter of 2026, alongside public administration and education and health and social services. That includes land transport, warehousing, and parts of the supply chain.

Manufacturing presents a different picture. Non-resident employment drove most of the economy's employment increase in the third quarter, mainly in construction and manufacturing. Manufacturing retrenchments rose from 670 to 870 in the second quarter. Construction's employment growth is largely work-permit hiring and does not translate directly into resident vacancies.

Vacancy figures for these sectors will be updated in the mid-December report.

How roles are usually advertised

In transport and warehousing, advertisements typically turn on licence class, shift pattern, and site. In manufacturing, production-operator roles and technician or engineer roles are advertised separately. With retrenchments higher here than in care or public services, contract length is often an important part of the posting.

Safety orientation and site access are standard parts of these roles. Requests to pay for a "site pass" before an employer has confirmed a role in writing fall outside ordinary hiring practice.

Details commonly found in postings

  • The licence or certificate required.
  • Shift hours, overtime practice, and the work site.
  • Whether the role is employment, daily-rated, or freelance.
  • Who issues any pass or permit, and whether any fee is involved.
