About

General information, written so it can be updated.

WorkNest SG is for people in Singapore who are preparing a job application and want the current industry picture in plain language.

What this is

The career path is a sequence: read the market, read one industry, compare skills and training, name the work arrangement, then apply through a channel you can check. The industry pages are briefings. They share what official statistics say and what an applicant can reasonably watch for.

We are independent. We are not the Ministry of Manpower, the Skills and Workforce Development Agency, CPF Board, TAFEP, or any employer. We do not list vacancies, recruit, screen, or place candidates.

How a page gets revised

A figure stays only while its source is the latest one we have checked. The review date is on the page. The update log says which release triggered the change. If you see a number that the next MOM report has replaced, tell us.

What we will not do

  • Promise a job, an interview, or a level of pay.
  • Describe ourselves as a partner or representative of a government agency.
  • Ask you to send a CV, NRIC, Singpass details, or money.
  • Estimate how much you could earn.
