About

Notes on the market, updated when the figures move.

WorkNest SG shares the current industry picture in Singapore in plain language: where resident hiring is holding, where it is quieter, and how work is usually arranged.

What this is

The notes cover the national labour figures, six industries, public training credits, work arrangements, and the contents of ordinary job advertisements. Each industry page follows an official release and carries the date of that release.

The site is independent of the Ministry of Manpower, the Skills and Workforce Development Agency, CPF Board, TAFEP, and employers. It lists no vacancies and handles no recruitment, screening, or placement.

How a page changes

A figure stays while its source is the latest one checked. The review date is on the page. The update log names the release behind the change. A number that a later MOM report has replaced can be flagged through the contact page.

Outside the scope of these notes

  • A promise of a job, an interview, or a level of pay.
  • Any description of this site as a partner or representative of a government agency.
  • CVs, NRIC numbers, Singpass details, or money.
  • An estimate of earnings.
