About WorkNest SG

An independent blog on how full-time, part-time, hybrid, online, contract, project-based, and freelance work are faring in Singapore's job market.

What we publish

WorkNest SG publishes a quarterly review of Singapore's labour market, a series on the main work arrangements, reports on six industries, and practical guides on job advertisements and training credits. The aim is to set each kind of work against the latest official data, in plain and accurate language.

Editorial standards

Figures are taken from published releases by the Ministry of Manpower, CPF Board, the Skills and Workforce Development Agency, and other public bodies, and each article lists its sources. Articles carry the date they were last reviewed, and revisions are recorded in the editor's log. Corrections are welcome through the contact page.

Independence

WorkNest SG is independent of the Ministry of Manpower, the Skills and Workforce Development Agency, CPF Board, TAFEP, and any employer. It is not an employment agency.

  • We do not list vacancies or recruit, screen, or place candidates.
  • We do not promise jobs, interviews, or levels of pay, and we do not estimate earnings.
  • We do not collect CVs, NRIC numbers, Singpass details, or payments.
