About

A guide to the market, updated when the figures move.

WorkNest SG explains how full-time, part-time, hybrid, online, contract, project-based, and freelance work are showing up in Singapore, against the latest industry figures.

What this is

The guide covers the national labour figures, six industries, public training credits, work arrangements, and what an ordinary job advertisement contains. Each industry page follows an official release and shows the date of that release.

The site is independent of the Ministry of Manpower, the Skills and Workforce Development Agency, CPF Board, TAFEP, and employers. It does not list vacancies, and it does not recruit, screen, or place anyone.

How a page changes

We leave a figure in place until a newer official source replaces it. The review date is on the page. The update log names the release behind a change. If a later MOM report has replaced a number you still see here, you can tell us on the contact page.

What this guide does not do

  • Promise a job, an interview, or a level of pay.
  • Present this site as a partner or representative of a government agency.
  • Take CVs, NRIC numbers, Singpass details, or money.
  • Estimate earnings.
