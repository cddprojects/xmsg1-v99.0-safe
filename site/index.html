Work types in Singapore · November 2026

Full-time, part-time, hybrid, online, contract, and freelance work.

These notes follow those six arrangements through the current market. Full-time and part-time hiring is still being supported in care, transport, and public services. Hybrid, online, contract, project-based, and freelance work show up more often in technology, finance, and professional services, where vacancies are thinner.
