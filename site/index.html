Editorial approach

Written from the official figures, and dated.

Every article on WorkNest SG is based on published releases from the Ministry of Manpower and other public agencies, names its sources, and carries the date it was last reviewed. When a new labour market report changes the picture, the affected articles are revised and the change is recorded in the editor's log.
