Common questions

Quick answers on work arrangements

Short answers drawn from the articles. Each topic is covered in more depth in the series.

Browse all articles
Does CPF apply to part-time work in Singapore?

Yes. A part-time employee is still an employee. For Singapore citizens and permanent residents, CPF applies once total wages exceed $50 a month. Where wages are above $50 and no more than $500, only the employer's share is payable.

What is the difference between contract and freelance work?

A fixed-term contract is usually employment with an end date, so Employment Act protections and CPF apply while it runs. Freelance work is self-employment under a contract for service, with MediSave and income tax handled under the self-employed rules.

Can employees ask for hybrid or flexible work?

Since 1 December 2024, the Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests set out how employees can make a formal written request. Employers are expected to consider it properly and respond within two months, and may decline on reasonable business grounds.

Are online job offers that ask for a deposit genuine?

Genuine employers do not ask workers to pay to start a job. Task-based schemes that ask for deposits to unlock payments are a well-known scam pattern. Suspected scams can be reported to the ScamShield helpline on 1799.

How often are the figures on WorkNest SG updated?

Articles are reviewed when the Ministry of Manpower publishes a new labour market release. The next is the Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected in mid-December 2026.
