Singapore job market · November 2026

Where hiring is holding, and where it has gone quiet.

Resident employment is still being supported in care, transport, and public services. Technology and finance are quieter, with fewer vacancies and more restructuring. These notes share that picture, and they change when the official figures change.

National snapshot

2.0%Overall unemployment in September 2026, unchanged from the month before.
+27,700Total employment growth in the third quarter of 2026. Most of it was non-resident, in construction and manufacturing.
42.3%Share of firms expecting to hire in the next three months, in the September 2026 poll. Up from August, and still described by MOM as selective.

Ministry of Manpower, Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026. Reviewed 19 November 2026.
