Industry reports

How six sectors are hiring

Each report covers one sector's latest figures and the work arrangements most often advertised there, from full-time and part-time shift work to hybrid, contract, and freelance roles.

  1. Care and communitySteadySteady resident demandHealth and social services remained one of the main sources of resident employment growth, with full-time and part-time shift work at the centre.→
  2. TechnologyCautiousHiring continues, on tighter termsInformation and communications vacancies fell by June and resident growth slowed in the third quarter. Hybrid, online, contract, and freelance roles remain part of the mix.→
  3. FinanceCautiousFewer vacancies, more selective hiringFinancial services recorded one of the two largest vacancy declines by June, and resident employment growth slowed in the third quarter.→
  4. Professional servicesMixedCooler after earlier gainsLegal, accounting, consulting, and engineering services added resident jobs in the second quarter, then slowed in the third. Project-based contracts are a large part of the picture.→
  5. Logistics and industryMixedA split pictureTransport and storage supported resident employment growth. Manufacturing's growth was mostly non-resident, alongside higher retrenchments.→
  6. Public and essential servicesSteadyWhere growth is concentratedPublic administration and education led resident employment growth in the third quarter. Hiring here tends to follow schemes and recruitment windows.→

Outlook labels summarise the latest official data for each sector. See the quarterly market review for the national picture.
