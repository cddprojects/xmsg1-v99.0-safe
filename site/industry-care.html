Industry note · Care and community

Health and social services are still part of the resident hiring story.

Steady resident demand

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

In the third quarter of 2026, resident employment growth was mainly supported by public administration and education, transportation and storage, and health and social services. Care sits in that last group: hospitals, clinics, eldercare, disability support, and community services.

The second-quarter report did not list health and social services among the sectors driving the rise in retrenchments. Those increases were concentrated in manufacturing, information and communications, and financial services. In June, entry-level PMET vacancies spanned sectors that included health and social services. MOM defines that group as vacancies with a minimum salary between $2,300 and $5,000.

Nursing, therapy, pharmacy, and social work carry registration or qualification rules. Support and administrative roles more often turn on shifts, language, and the centre where the work happens.

How the work is usually described

Advertisements in this sector usually state the workplace, the shift pattern, and whether the role is employment or a contract for service. Part-time care work under a contract of service remains employment. CPF for Singapore citizens and permanent residents in that kind of employment applies when monthly wages are more than $50. The current table is published by CPF Board.

Where a role is regulated, the meaning of "registered" or "enrolled" is set out on the regulator's own site. Descriptions of care roles commonly mention shift attendance, records, and work with families.

Details that often appear in advertisements

  • Registration or enrolment, where the role is regulated.
  • Shift pattern, including nights, weekends, and how far ahead the roster is published.
  • Employment or freelance, and which party pays CPF.
  • Site location. Community roles are often spread across several centres.

National figures: MOM Labour Market Advance Release, third quarter 2026, and Labour Market Report, second quarter 2026. This note shares that public picture.
