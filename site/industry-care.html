Industry briefing · Care and community

Health and social services are still part of the resident hiring story.

Steady resident demand

Updated 19 November 2026Next official sector and vacancy release: MOM Labour Market Report 3Q 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The situation

In the third quarter of 2026, resident employment growth was mainly supported by public administration and education, transportation and storage, and health and social services. Care sits in that last group: hospitals, clinics, eldercare, disability support, and community services.

The second-quarter report did not list health and social services among the sectors driving the rise in retrenchments. Those increases were concentrated in manufacturing, information and communications, and financial services. In June, entry-level PMET vacancies spanned sectors that included health and social services. MOM defines that group as vacancies with a minimum salary between $2,300 and $5,000.

A sector that is still adding resident jobs is not an easy sector. Nursing, therapy, pharmacy, and social work have registration or qualification rules. Support and administrative roles often turn on shifts, language, and whether you can work a particular site.

If you are applying

Read the advertisement for the workplace, the shift pattern, and whether the role is employment or a contract for service. Part-time care work under a contract of service is still employment. CPF rules for citizen and permanent-resident employees apply above $50 a month. Confirm the current rule with CPF Board rather than with a recruiter in a chat app.

If the role needs professional registration, check the regulator's own site for what "registered" or "enrolled" means before you pay for a course that claims to qualify you.

Applications that lead with reliable shift attendance, documentation, and working with families tend to match how these roles are described. A generic technology CV does not substitute for that, and a care CV does not need to pretend otherwise.

What to check in the advertisement

  • Registration or enrolment status, if the role is regulated.
  • Shift pattern, including nights and weekends, and how far ahead the roster is published.
  • Whether the contract is employment or freelance, and who pays CPF.
  • Site location and travel time. Community roles are often spread across several centres.
