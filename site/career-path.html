Overview

Notes on a market that is moving in more than one direction.

The national total is up. Resident hiring is concentrated in essential and public services, while technology, finance, and professional services are quieter. The notes below stay with that split.

Updated 19 November 2026The next official sector and vacancy release is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

The national picture

A rising employment total can be mostly work-permit hiring in construction and manufacturing, while resident hiring in technology or finance is quiet. The national note keeps those two measures apart: total employment rose by 27,700 in the third quarter of 2026, and most of that increase was non-resident.

Sectors are also on different cycles. Care, transport and storage, and public services supported resident employment. Information and communications, financial services, and professional services had subdued resident growth in the same quarter.

Industries

The industry notes stay with one sector each. Scheduling, customer communication, records, and basic data work show up in more than one of them. The label on each note — steady, mixed, or cautious — follows the latest official release for that sector.

Skills and training

Advertisements and job titles describe overlapping tasks. A skill that shows up across several current advertisements is the sort of gap public catalogues are built around. A skill that shows up once is a narrower case. The skills note covers SkillsFuture Credit and the public course list, including the amounts and which courses they apply to.

Work arrangements

The word "flexible" in an advertisement can mean hybrid days, shift work, or freelance delivery. Those arrangements carry different CPF, leave, and tax patterns. The work arrangements note sets out how part-time, contract, hybrid, and freelance work are usually defined in Singapore.

Applications

Advertisements usually name an employer, hours, a workplace, and a pay basis. Some messages also ask for a fee, a Singpass login, a one-time password, or a conversation that exists only in a messaging app. The applications note describes both the ordinary contents and those other requests.

Dates on the notes

Each industry page carries the date it was last aligned to an official release. The update log records which release that was. The next fuller sector report is expected in mid-December 2026.
