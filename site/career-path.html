Overview

The market is moving in more than one direction.

Full-time and part-time hiring is concentrated in essential and public services. Hybrid, online, contract, project-based, and freelance work are easier to spot in technology, finance, and professional services, where the quarter is quieter. The guide below stays with that split.

Updated 19 November 2026The next official look at sectors and vacancies is the MOM Labour Market Report for the third quarter of 2026, expected in mid-December 2026.

The national picture

A rising employment total can be mostly work-permit hiring in construction and manufacturing, while resident hiring in technology or finance is quiet. The national picture keeps those two measures apart: total employment rose by 27,700 in the third quarter of 2026, and most of that increase was non-resident.

Sectors are also on different cycles. Care, transport and storage, and public services supported resident employment. Information and communications, financial services, and professional services had slower resident growth in the same quarter.

Industries

Each industry guide stays with one sector. Scheduling, customer conversations, records, and basic data work show up in more than one of them. The label on each page — steady, mixed, or cautious — follows the latest official release for that sector.

Skills and training

Job titles often overlap. A skill that appears in several current advertisements is the kind of thing public course lists are built around. A skill that appears once is more specific to that role. The skills and training guide covers SkillsFuture Credit and the public course list, including the amounts and which courses they apply to.

Work arrangements

"Flexible" in an advertisement can mean hybrid days, shift work, or freelance delivery. Those are not the same thing for CPF, leave, or tax. The work types guide explains how full-time, part-time, hybrid, online, contract, and freelance work are usually set up in Singapore.

Applications

An advertisement usually names an employer, the hours, a workplace, and how pay is calculated. Some messages also ask for a fee, a Singpass login, a one-time password, or a chat that exists only in a messaging app. The applications guide covers both what is ordinary and what is worth treating with care.

Dates on the pages

Each industry page shows the date it was last checked against an official release. The update log records which release that was. The next fuller sector report is expected in mid-December 2026.

Connect by SWDA, at the Lifelong Learning Institute, is a public centre where you can talk about careers and skills. This guide is independent of that service.
