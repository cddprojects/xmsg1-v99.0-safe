Career path

A path for the application, not a placement service.

Use the steps in order if you are starting out, or jump to the one you are stuck on. Nothing here submits an application or contacts an employer for you.

Updated 19 November 2026Next official sector and vacancy release: MOM Labour Market Report 3Q 2026, expected mid-December 2026.

01. See where the market is moving

A rising employment total can be mostly work-permit hiring in construction and manufacturing, while the resident market in technology or finance is quiet. Read the national update first so a headline does not send you into a crowded search.

Write down two things: the kind of work you can start within your current notice period, and the hours you can actually give. The market page will not choose for you. It stops you treating every sector as if it were on the same cycle.

02. Read one industry briefing

Open the industry list and read the sector you are considering, plus one adjacent sector. Skills such as scheduling, customer communication, records, and basic data work show up in more than one place. The briefing tells you whether resident hiring there is steady, mixed, or cautious right now.

03. Match skills before you buy training

List tasks you have done, not only job titles. Compare that list with two or three current advertisements in the sector. Gaps that appear in several ads are worth a course. A gap that appears once is not a reason to enrol. The skills and training page covers SkillsFuture Credit, public catalogues, and questions to ask before you pay.

04. Decide the arrangement

An advertisement that says "flexible" can mean hybrid days, shift work, or freelance delivery. Those have different CPF, leave, and tax consequences. Use work options to name the arrangement you want, then check whether the advertisement is actually offering it.

05. Apply through a channel you can verify

The applying page covers how to read an advertisement, what to put in a short application, and the requests that are a reason to stop: upfront fees, Singpass or one-time passwords, and contact that exists only in a messaging app.

06. Keep a record, then revisit the date

Note where you applied, the arrangement offered, and the date. When the next labour report lands, reread the industry page rather than assuming last quarter's picture still holds. The update log shows what changed on this site and why.

If you want in-person career or skills advice, Connect by SWDA at the Lifelong Learning Institute is a public service centre. WorkNest SG is not part of that service and cannot book it for you.
